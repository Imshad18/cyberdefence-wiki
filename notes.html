Workato Integration Notes

This page collects a few quick notes on connecting Workato recipes across Slack, Google Calendar, and GitHub. Recipes use triggers and actions to move data between connected apps without custom code.

Trigger basics

A trigger starts a recipe when an event happens in a connected app — a new Slack message, a calendar event, a pushed commit. Actions then run in sequence against the other connectors.
