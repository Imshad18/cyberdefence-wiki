What can be returned

  • Unused items in their original packaging
  • Items that arrived damaged or faulty
  • Incorrect items sent in error

What cannot be returned

  • Clearance and final-sale items
  • Custom or made-to-order products
  • Gift cards and digital downloads

How to start a return

Email our support team with your order number and a short description of the issue. We’ll send a prepaid shipping label within two business days.

Refund timing. Once your return reaches our warehouse, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. Your bank may take a few additional days to post the credit.

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement.
